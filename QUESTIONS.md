## Czech Republic

### C1. Pilsner
- **ES:** El estilo de cerveza pilsner, como la peruana Pilsen Callao, nació en 1842 en una ciudad checa. ¿En cuál?
- **Answers:** Plzeň / Praga / Brno / Ostrava
- **Explicación:** La pilsner toma su nombre de Plzeň, que en alemán se llama Pilsen. Allí se hizo en 1842 la primera lager clara.
- **EN:** The pilsner style of beer, like Peru's Pilsen Callao, was born in 1842 in a Czech city. Which one?
- **Answers:** Plzeň / Prague / Brno / Ostrava
- **Explanation:** Pilsner takes its name from Plzeň, called Pilsen in German. The first pale lager was made there in 1842.
- **Sources:** [Pilsner, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilsner) (read); [Pilsen Callao trademark, "Peruvian Pilsen Beer"](https://trademark.justia.com/732/37/beer-pilsen-callao-peruvian-pilsen-beerproduct-of-peru-since-1863-73237648.html) (search only)

### J2. Mochi (new 2026-11-12, from the project owner's friend)
- **Scene:** filled daifuku on a dish (left), plain mochi toasting on a grill (right). The friend gave the answer: only the right one.

- **EN:** The Japanese immigrant Yokichi Nouchi is remembered as the first mayor of a Peruvian town. Which one?
- **Answers:** Machu Picchu / Cañete / Ollantaytambo / Nasca
- **Explanation:** Nouchi came from Fukushima and helped develop tourism in the town below the Inca citadel.
- **Sources:** [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/mundo/actualidad/historia-japones-primer-alcalde-machu-picchu-440182) (read); [Discover Nikkei](https://5dn.org:443/en/journal/2017/8/30/yokichi-nouchi/) (search only: Fukushima, tourism). His exact first year in office varies (1938–39 as municipal agent, mayor by 1948), so the text says "is remembered as" and gives no year.
